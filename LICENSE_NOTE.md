# About this ledger

This repository is the public, timestamped scoreboard for Deal Makers Digest.

- Each row is a corporate deal, scored the day it was announced. The commit history is the
  proof of timing and cannot be backdated.
- The scores and outcomes are public. The scoring methodology (the exact rubric and models)
  is proprietary and is NOT published here.
- Nothing here is investment advice. These are research observations about historical market
  reactions, exploratory and small-sample. Quarterly report-backs include the misses.
